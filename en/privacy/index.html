Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Introduction

Watira is an app for tracking daily tasks and habits, developed and run by Abdulrahman Alyami in Saudi Arabia. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, where it is kept, and what control you have over it.

By using the app you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

Information we collect

Without signing in: your tasks, habits and settings stay on your device only and are not sent to any server.

When you create an account: your email address and password (stored hashed; we cannot read it), the name you type and the form of address you choose.

Your content in the app: the titles of your tasks and habits, their dates, status, categories and reminder times, the daily record your streak is counted from, and settings such as language, appearance and the first day of the week.

Simple usage numbers: how many times the app is opened and how long it is used per day, per account. We do not record which screens you visit or what you type.

We do not collect your location, contacts or photos, and we use no advertising trackers.

How we use it

To run the app: saving your tasks and habits and syncing them between your devices, counting your streak, and emailing you a confirmation or password-reset code.

To improve the app: we look at aggregate numbers such as how many people are active and average use.

We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising, and show no ads in the app.

Where it is kept and how it is protected

Account data is stored on the servers of our hosting provider, Supabase, in Germany (European Union). The connection between the app and the server is encrypted, each account can reach only its own data, and your sign-in session is stored encrypted on your device.

We do what we reasonably can to protect your data, but no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Sharing and third-party services

We share your data with no one except the service providers that run the app, and only as far as that requires:

Supabase: database hosting and sign-in.

Resend: sending account emails (confirmation and password-reset codes); only your email address passes through it.

Apple: completing purchases and subscriptions through the App Store. We never receive or store your card details.

We may disclose information if a law or a competent authority requires it.

Transfer outside Saudi Arabia

Because the providers above operate outside Saudi Arabia, your account data is stored and processed outside the Kingdom. By using the app and creating an account you agree to this.

Notifications and device permissions

If you turn reminders on, the app uses your device's local notifications; they do not pass through our servers. You can turn them off at any time in the app's settings or your device's settings.

Exporting or importing your data and sharing a picture of your progress happen on your device and only when you ask. The file or picture stays with you; we have no access to it.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you learn that a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Retention and account deletion

We keep your account data for as long as the account exists.

You can permanently delete your account and all of its data from the server inside the app: Settings, then Account, then Delete account. Deletion cannot be undone.

Removing the app from your device deletes the data stored on the device; the account data stays on the server until you delete the account.

Your rights

You can see and correct your data inside the app, get a copy of it with “Export my data”, delete it by deleting your account, and withdraw your consent by stopping use of the app and deleting your account. For any other request, write to us.

Subscriptions and payments

Purchases are made through the app store, which handles payment under its own policy. We receive only what shows the state of your subscription (subscribed or not, the plan and its end date) so that we can unlock the features for you.

Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, including the Personal Data Protection Law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. We change the “Last updated” date above and, if the change is significant, tell you inside the app.
